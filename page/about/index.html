Why #anooptube

The name started as my YouTube handle and stuck. Every post on this blog has a companion video or playlist, and the goal is the same for both: make complex topics approachable for developers at every level.

How I teach

I learn by building, so the tutorials do too. You get real code you can run, the reasoning behind it, and the gotchas I hit along the way, not just the happy path.
